# 📊 Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

An end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing retail performance across Indian cities (2020–2024).  
Designed to support data-driven decisions on products, promotions, and regional trends.

---

## 🔹 Project Summary

- Analyzed **3,510 orders** and **7,100 units sold**
- Generated insights from **₹122M total sales** and **₹12.2M profit**
- Built a **multi-page interactive dashboard**
- Enabled filtering by **product, promotion, customer, and date**
- Delivered **actionable insights for business decision-making**

## 🔸 Key Business Insights 💭

**1. Revenue is concentrated in premium electronics**  
Products like iPhone, MacBook, and Sony TV dominate total sales.  
→ Focus on premium customer segments and upselling  

---

**2. Low-value products contribute minimal revenue**  
FMCG items (toothpaste, soap, etc.) have negligible impact.  
→ Bundle, reprice, or remove low-performing SKUs  

---

**3. Promotions drive high-ticket sales**  
Weekend Flash Sale (₹23K avg discount) and Clearance Sale (₹18K) are highest.  
→ Discounts are primarily used to move premium inventory  

---

**4. Sales are regionally concentrated**  
Higher order volume in northern and central India.  
→ Opportunity for regional expansion
